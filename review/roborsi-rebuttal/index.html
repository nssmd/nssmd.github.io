W1 · 基线比较不公平

审稿意见
Unfair baselines. RoboRSI keeps learning on the evaluated tasks, while the baselines do not. As a result, the evaluation shows the gain from self-improvement itself, but not RoboRSI's advantage over other RSI systems or approaches.
Q1: If possible, could the authors compare RoboRSI with related robotic RSI systems (e.g., ASPIRE, ENPIRE, or EmbodiSkill), or with a simple RSI variant built on one of the baselines?
建议回复

We thank the reviewer for this important point. We agree that the original comparison mainly measures the benefit of self-improvement over non-learning agents. To isolate RoboRSI's advantage over other self-improving designs, we added two comparisons under a matched learning budget (same number of learning episodes and the same LLM backbone):

(1) Self-improving baselines. We built an RSI variant on top of the strongest baseline (OpenETA + reflection-based code repair with flat revision), and we additionally compared with [ASPIRE / ENPIRE / EmbodiSkill，按实际能跑通的写]. RoboRSI outperforms the best of these by [X] pp on average across the four benchmarks (new Table [X]).

(2) Learn-then-freeze protocol. We separated learning from evaluation: the skill library is frozen after learning on a training split and evaluated on held-out tasks / perturbed initial states. RoboRSI achieves [X]% vs. [X]% for the RSI baseline, showing that the gains transfer and are not due to continued learning on test tasks.

需要补充的实验
  • 在最强基线（OpenETA）上搭一个自我改进版本：失败后反思并修改代码，用 flat revision，学习预算和 LLM 与 RoboRSI 一致。
  • 尝试复现或接入 ASPIRE、ENPIRE、EmbodiSkill 中至少一个；跑不通的话在回复里说明原因。
  • 先学习后冻结：在训练任务上学习，冻结技能库，再在没见过的任务或换了初始状态的任务上测试；RoboRSI 和自我改进基线都按这个流程跑。

The remaining gap of [X] pp comes from failures that a confirmation step cannot fix, such as [按失败分析填写，如 grasp pose 错误、技能缺失], which RoboRSI repairs through skill revision.
